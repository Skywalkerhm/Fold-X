Research behind the work

Three public preprints document the team's research on inference quality, efficiency evaluation, and mathematical task performance. They provide research context, rather than certification of the current Fold-X API or the seven demos.

- Virtual MoE — inference quality and reliability: studies inference quality and hallucination mitigation in a small-model setting. Its reported findings are specific to the evaluated model and tasks.

- SemanticFold — evaluating efficiency and capability together: examines whether different capability measures are preserved under compression. The measures can move in different directions; a gain in one does not establish a gain in all.

- Task presentation and execution paths shape LLM mathematical performance: studies how task presentation and available information affect mathematical answers, with paired comparisons showing task-dependent contributions.

These are arXiv preprints, not evidence of peer-review acceptance or independent product certification. Consult each paper for its experimental conditions and limitations. No unpublished implementation details are included here.

Go

CASE 01 / Intelligence and strategic judgment

Read the board. Choose the next move.

Fold-X Flash plays White against DeepSeek on a 19×19 Go board. The game shows a sequence of decisions that depend on the position, the available moves, and their consequences.

The starting point is strategic judgment: understanding a situation within a clear set of rules and choosing how to act.

Where this can help · A demonstration of the kind of judgment needed to compare options and weigh immediate gains against longer-term consequences.

What the recording shows
  • The final interface estimates a lead of 80.5 points for White. The specific DeepSeek version was not provided.
  • The video preserves the order of play and focuses on the board and score display.

Reading the speed claims · Playback is accelerated to 5× for viewing. This is not a measurement of model response speed.

This is an interface estimate from one game, not an independently adjudicated result, a multi-game win rate, or a general intelligence benchmark.

Super Mario

CASE 02 / Fast, effective action selection

Act at the right moment.

With enemies moving, terrain changing, and limited time to act, Fold-X repeatedly chooses whether to move, jump, or wait. The game runs on the left; the panel on the right shows the current state, selected action, and candidate scores.

Strategy now meets timing: as the environment changes, a useful decision must become an effective action quickly enough to matter.

Where this can help · Relevant to game agents, interactive applications, and action selection in systems with continuous feedback.

What the recording shows
  • The recording shows continuous play across several levels and ends during World 4-3.

The recording illustrates action timing and outcomes. It does not establish a success rate across repeated runs, completion of the entire game, or control from raw pixels alone.

Robot courier

CASE 03 / Continuous decisions in complex environments

Adapt to the world as it changes.

In an Unreal Engine delivery simulation, the robot receives a destination while environmental elements, pedestrians, and vehicles are placed randomly. Fold-X uses the information available at each step to decide whether to move, wait, or adjust its action.

Beyond timing a single action, the robot must consider several factors together: its goal, pedestrians, vehicles, signals, and whether the route is clear.

Where this can help · An exploration of how robots could interact with the world: observe a changing situation, respond in pursuit of a goal, and adjust as new information arrives.

What the recording shows
  • Project materials identify the virtual robot asset as a Unitree G9 model. This naming follows the project documentation.
  • The recording includes waiting when too little green-light time remains, stopping at the curb on red, and proceeding after conditions change.
  • According to the project team, no pretraining was performed specifically for this delivery scenario or route. This does not mean the underlying model was never trained.

Reading the speed claims · The video retains its original playback speed. Recording length is not per-decision latency.

This is an Unreal Engine simulation. It does not establish deployment on a physical robot, completion of the full delivery, or human-equivalent behavior.

Traffic signal coordination

CASE 04 / Coordinating multiple decision points

Keep 62 intersections working together.

Fold-X participates in signal decisions across 62 intersections as simulated traffic conditions change. Releasing traffic at one junction affects downstream roads, so each local choice needs to be considered in the context of the wider network.

The scope expands from one acting agent to multiple connected decision points, each with local demands and consequences for the rest of the system.

Where this can help · A functional demonstration relevant to network coordination, resource allocation, and ongoing scheduling decisions.

What the recording shows
  • The simulation shows continuing decisions across 62 intersections as traffic conditions evolve.
  • The interface labels this run as an invalid comparison. No traffic-efficiency improvement is claimed from it.

Reading the speed claims · The recording has not been further accelerated. The application itself displays a 16× replay speed, which cannot be used to infer decision throughput.

This is a simulation and replay, not evidence of a city deployment or global optimality. It also does not establish that all 62 decisions are made in a single API call.

From noticing details to interpreting beauty.

The art demo explores how lines, color, composition, and spatial relationships shape the mood of paintings and photographs. The antique demo applies close visual observation to objects, distinguishing visible clues from questions that need further examination.

Recognition and comparison lead to interpretation: how do details work together to create a visual impression, and what tentative conclusions can they support?

Frequently asked questions

What is Fold-X?

A multimodal decision model for judgments, choices, and scoring across text and images, designed for accuracy, speed, and efficiency. Flash-1.3 is currently available through an API.

What should I try first?

Classification, condition checks, candidate selection, scoring, or image-condition screening. Start with a task that has clear inputs, outputs, and acceptance criteria, and test representative samples.

Can I run the seven demos directly through the API?

The API supplies decisions. Video search, games, robotics, and traffic applications also need frame extraction, environment connections, action execution, or other integration. A demo is not a promise that the complete application is a standard service.

What supports the Chinese-language capability?

Testing covers word meaning, sentence relationships, ambiguous words, and rhetorical knowledge. Flash in its deep configuration achieved a 93.33% macro-average across four C-SEM public-set tasks totaling 1,200 questions in a team-run test. This is not accuracy across all Chinese-language tasks.

Are accuracy, speed, and efficiency guaranteed on every task?

They are product goals, not unconditional guarantees. Quality depends on the task; model timing differs from end-to-end API latency; costs must cover the complete workflow. Each result includes its test conditions and limitations.

Is a returned score the probability that the answer is correct?

No. Scores help compare options or apply thresholds, but should not be treated as validated correctness probabilities. Evaluate them on your own data and use human review where appropriate.

How do I get access and how is it billed?

Use the HTTPS text or image APIs. Current access supports point-based and time-based allowances; pricing and quotas are confirmed during onboarding. Contact the founders for a trial, or use GitHub Issues for general product questions.

Are code, weights, or customer records published here?

This repository presents product capabilities, evaluation summaries, and demonstrations. It does not publish core implementation, model weights, keys, or raw customer data. Discuss business data and handling requirements privately.
