Research behind the work

Three public preprints document the team's research on inference quality, efficiency evaluation, and mathematical task performance. They provide research context, rather than certification of the current Fold-X API or the eight demos.

- Virtual MoE — inference quality and reliability: studies inference quality and hallucination mitigation in a small-model setting. Its reported findings are specific to the evaluated model and tasks.

- SemanticFold — evaluating efficiency and capability together: examines whether different capability measures are preserved under compression. The measures can move in different directions; a gain in one does not establish a gain in all.

- Task presentation and execution paths shape LLM mathematical performance: studies how task presentation and available information affect mathematical answers, with paired comparisons showing task-dependent contributions.

These are arXiv preprints, not evidence of peer-review acceptance or independent product certification. Consult each paper for its experimental conditions and limitations. No unpublished implementation details are included here.

CASE 03 / Decisions in complex environments

Robot delivery: account for uncertainty, keep the task moving.

In an Unreal Engine city simulation, Fold-X makes ongoing decisions using weather, pedestrians, vehicles, signals, and task progress, completing three deliveries and returning to the station after each. Randomized changes in the scenario—and their effects on visibility, passage conditions, and interactions—inform ongoing decisions.

Beyond choosing a timely action, the robot follows a complete task cycle in changing conditions: depart, assess the situation, deliver, return, and begin the next order.

Where this can help · Explore continuous task decisions for campus delivery, inspection, and service robots: adjust actions toward a goal as conditions change and interactions interrupt the journey.

What the recording shows
  • Complete three-order recording: three deliveries and three returns. Logs record no pedestrian or vehicle contact and one static curb contact.
  • Separate curb-handling check: one delivery and return, one proactive step-up, and no recorded contact. These results apply only to this separate run.
  • The randomized scenario mechanism and the flow of observed conditions into decisions were checked. No random starting point was triggered in this run, and aggregate results across random seeds are not yet available.

Both videos play at 5× speed. The full recording is about 20 minutes, shown in about 4 minutes; the separate check is about 2 minutes 24 seconds, shown in about 29 seconds. Playback duration is neither actual task duration nor per-decision latency.

This is a single Unreal Engine simulation run, not physical-robot deployment or a success-rate study across randomized trials. Routes are chosen from a predefined set of candidates; this is not unrestricted autonomous exploration.

CASE 08 / Fine-detail visual quality inspection

Visual quality inspection: spot potential issues and review the details

The application supports bedding cleanliness checks by flagging fine, low-contrast hair candidates among fabric textures and folds, marking their locations, and opening enlarged views. The focus is visual quality inspection: spotting potential issues, locating the evidence, and supporting human review.

The progression moves from understanding a scene to checking its quality: use small foreign-material candidates to identify areas that need attention and provide visual evidence for review.

Application potential · Turn details that require frame-by-frame searching into candidate locations for human review and records, with potential pilots in linen inspection and textile foreign-material screening.

Observations and evidence limits
  • The demo first limits inspection to bedding, then scans the video and displays candidate results.

Frequently asked questions

What is Fold-X?

A multimodal decision model for judgments, choices, and scoring across text and images, designed for accuracy, speed, and efficiency. Flash-1.3 is currently available through an API.

What should I try first?

Classification, condition checks, candidate selection, scoring, or image-condition screening. Start with a task that has clear inputs, outputs, and acceptance criteria, and test representative samples.

Can I run the eight demos directly through the API?

The API supplies decisions. Video search, games, robotics, and traffic applications also need frame extraction, environment connections, action execution, or other integration. A demo is not a promise that the complete application is a standard service.

What supports the Chinese-language capability?

Testing covers word meaning, sentence relationships, ambiguous words, and rhetorical knowledge. Flash in its deep configuration achieved a 93.33% macro-average across four C-SEM public-set tasks totaling 1,200 questions in a team-run test. This is not accuracy across all Chinese-language tasks.

Are accuracy, speed, and efficiency guaranteed on every task?

They are product goals, not unconditional guarantees. Quality depends on the task; model timing differs from end-to-end API latency; costs must cover the complete workflow. Each result includes its test conditions and limitations.

Is a returned score the probability that the answer is correct?

No. Scores help compare options or apply thresholds, but should not be treated as validated correctness probabilities. Evaluate them on your own data and use human review where appropriate.

How do I get access and how is it billed?

Use the HTTPS text or image APIs. Current access supports point-based and time-based allowances; pricing and quotas are confirmed during onboarding. Contact the founders for a trial, or use GitHub Issues for general product questions.

Are code, weights, or customer records published here?

This repository presents product capabilities, evaluation summaries, and demonstrations. It does not publish core implementation, model weights, keys, or raw customer data. Discuss business data and handling requirements privately.
